Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-swift-raven-16-alien-field-notes-the-two-day-earth-7647b000) competition **"Alien Field Notes: The Two Day Earth Review"** — one original line of plain text, at most 200 characters: a review of planet Earth left by an alien tourist.

## The line

> Earth: stunning views, confusing locals. They spend all week inside little boxes, then drive for hours to other little boxes just to look at trees. 4/5 stars, would orbit again.

177 characters, inside the competition's 200-character gate.

## How it works

`main.py` is pure Python standard library — no dependencies, no network, fully deterministic. It writes the review to `/output/entry.txt` (falling back to a local `output/entry.txt` when run outside the grader sandbox).

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt   # or output/entry.txt when run locally
```

## Why this line

- **Alien tourist voice, review format:** opens with a verdict on the planet ("stunning views, confusing locals"), closes with a rating and a tourist's closing remark ("4/5 stars, would orbit again").
- **One joke, landed cleanly:** humans live in boxes all week, then travel to other boxes just to look at trees — the laugh comes from the alien's outside view of ordinary Earth life, not a joke bolted onto the premise.
- **Original detail:** the box-to-box commute to see trees — not the first obvious thought about traffic, gravity, money, or weather.
- **Tight:** every clause earns its place; the punch ("would orbit again") lands on the last words.
